What the panel does

The repetitive part of a podcast edit, done on your own timeline.

Cuts to the speaker

Analyze shows who is on which camera, and you can change it. From a solo show up to 8 speakers, with optional wide shots for long monologues and moments when people talk at once.

Trims silence and breaths

Choose the shortest silence to cut, which pauses stay and how much air remains around words. 0 switches a rule off, and your numbers are remembered.

Finds failed takes

Find retakes lists failed takes, stumbles and off-camera chatter. In each group you choose which attempt stays — KEEP / CUT — before anything leaves the timeline.

Picks Reels and a teaser

Write a brief in your own words or pick a focus — Viral, Insight, Humour, Story, Debate, Quote. The chosen reels are laid out on a copy of your sequence, vertical 1080×1920 if you want.

Speaks your language

The panel works in Azərbaycan, English and Русский. Transcription follows the spoken language; the AI's notes — retake reasons, reel titles — come in the language you pick.

Help built in

Ask AI answers questions about using Cutterly, 30 a day. Report a problem sends us your message with the panel version and recent errors attached.

Nothing is cut behind your back

The panel suggests; the edit stays yours.

  • A «… - Backup» copy of the sequence is made before the edit — and Cmd+Z still works.
  • Colour markers: red — to delete, green — the take that stays, orange — doubtful, your call.
  • «Cut + colour — I'll review» cuts and colours without deleting; you delete after a look. Sensitivity: Safe, Balanced, Aggressive.
  • Finding fragments never touches your timeline; reels are built on a copy and the original stays as it was.

How it works

  1. Lay out the timeline

    Put each camera on V1, V2… linked to its own audio. The panel only touches tracks tied to cameras — everything else stays put.

  2. Analyze

    Export Now sends the timeline's audio, or pick a WAV with Browse. The server works out who speaks when; a long podcast takes 5–15 minutes and keeps going with Premiere in the background.

  3. Create Multi-Cam Edit

    Camera edit, silence trim and AI cleanup in one pass, with the Backup copy made automatically.

Your video stays on your Mac

Only the exported audio goes to the Cutterly server for analysis, and it is deleted automatically within 12 hours. Error reports drop file names and paths before they are sent — and you can switch them off in Settings.

Questions

Does it work on Windows?

Yes — Windows 10/11, alongside macOS. The download page has install steps for both.

Where does my audio go?

The audio sent for analysis is kept on the server temporarily and deleted within 12 hours. Your video files never leave your computer.

Can I undo the edit?

Yes — Cmd+Z, or carry on from the «… - Backup» copy made before the cut.

What happens when my hours run out?

New analyses do not start until your hours renew on your subscription day, or until you move to a larger plan.

How do I cancel?

Just don't renew — access stays until the end of the paid month and nothing is charged automatically. See the refund policy.
